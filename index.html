About

I am an Assistant Professor and independent principal investigator in the School of Mechanics and Engineering Science at Peking University.

My research lies at the intersection of wave mechanics, active materials, acoustofluidics, and bioengineering . I am particularly interested in creating dynamically programmable systems for controlling waves and matter across spatial and temporal scales.

Current research directions include acoustofluidics and micro/nano manipulation, acoustic bioelectronics, active metamaterials, topological mechanics, and spatiotemporal wave modulation.

Research

03

Active Metamaterials

Odd constitutive properties, active feedback, nonreciprocity, exceptional points, and non-Hermitian wave dynamics.

04

Topological & Spatiotemporal Wave Mechanics

Topological states, space-time modulation, temporal interfaces, frequency conversion, and programmable elastic and acoustic waves.

01

Acoustofluidics & Micro/Nano Manipulation

Dynamic acoustic fields for controlling particles, cells, droplets, and micro/nanoscale matter in fluids.

02

Acoustic Bioelectronics

Acoustic sensing, actuation, mechanobiology, wearable systems, and interfaces between electronics and biological systems.
