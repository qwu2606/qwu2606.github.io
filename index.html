About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanics and Engineering Science at Peking University.

My research lies at the intersection of wave mechanics, active materials, acoustofluidics, and bioengineering . We develop physical principles and engineered systems for dynamically controlling waves and matter across multiple spatial and temporal scales.

Current research interests include acoustofluidics and acoustic manipulation, acoustic bioelectronics, active and non-Hermitian metamaterials, spatiotemporal modulation, and topological wave mechanics.

Research

01

Acoustofluidics & Micro/Nano Manipulation

Dynamic acoustic fields for manipulating particles, cells, droplets, and micro/nanoscale matter in fluids, with an emphasis on programmable and spatiotemporal control.

02

Acoustic Bioelectronics

Acoustic sensing and actuation platforms that connect electronics, acoustics, and biological systems for mechanobiology, diagnostics, therapeutics, and wearable technologies.

03

Active & Non-Hermitian Metamaterials

Active constitutive control, odd material properties, nonreciprocity, exceptional-point physics, and non-Hermitian wave phenomena in mechanical and acoustic systems.

04

Spatiotemporal & Topological Wave Mechanics

Space-time modulation, frequency conversion, synthetic dimensions, topological states, and programmable transport of elastic and acoustic waves.
